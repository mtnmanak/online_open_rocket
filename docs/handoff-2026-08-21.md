## Why rename (established this session, Eric concurs)

1. **"Online" is false advertising in reverse** — the live meta description literally
   ends "offline-capable" while the title says Online.
2. **"OpenRocket" exceeds nominative fair use** — research found no registered mark and
   no fork-naming policy, but ~17 years of continuous use gives the OpenRocket project
   common-law rights; GPL grants zero trademark permission (24.12 LICENSE.TXT's only §7
   term covers data-file bundling); using their name as OUR product identifier implies
   sponsorship — the exact issue behind every precedent rename (Iceweasel, LibreOffice,
   MariaDB, Jellyfin). Nominative use keeps everything we need: the
   "OpenRocket-derived physics" tagline, "based on OpenRocket 24.12", .ork
   compatibility claims, GPL attribution.
3. **Timing is the cheapest it will ever be**: invite unsent, site public one week,
   repo public one day, installed-PWA population ≈ a handful of known testers.

## Naming brainstorm — state and shortlist

Process: superpowers:brainstorming. Eric's choices: lanes = evocative flight word +
coined unique word + MMR hybrid (descriptive compound rejected); tone = **serious sim
cred + clean/modern-minimal** (explicitly NOT playful/folksy); not tied to Mountain Man
Rockets branding, MMR initials allowed; naming THIS app only, not an umbrella.

Two workflow rounds: 92 candidates generated, 24 web-vetted (strict: any
rocketry/aero owner or prominent software product = high risk).

**Key lesson: every glamorous aerospace word is owned, usually inside hobby rocketry
itself.** Kills: Mach (Mach 1 Rocketry), MaxQ (Max Q Rockets), Zenith (Quest "Zenith
II" kit), Caliber (LOC "Caliber ISP" + calibre e-book homophone), Ascent (Ascent
Aerospace + hobby pad electronics), Apsis (APSIS One + two aero firms), Velox (Meta
Velox + VeloX Aerospace), Ogive (Indian defense-sim company + generic in our own UI),
Strake (3 software cos + AeroTech part), Longeron (UK aerospace group), Altidyne
(confusable with Avidyne avionics — textbook 2(d)), plus pre-kills Kutta/Deltav/
Impulse/Vernier/Nominal/Apex/Vector/Boost.

**Survivors (LOW collision risk, search-verified):**

| Name | Construction | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| **Midchord** | Real Barrowman fin-geometry term | Thin/descriptive-ish mark; bare-word SEO buried in aero literature; NOT a label in our own UI (unlike Azimuth/Semispan, which died for that) |
| **Ogivane** | ogive + vane, coined | Pronunciation wobble (oh-JIH-vane); "ogive"=warhead in French (mild) |
| **Apsivex** | apsis + -vex, coined | Emptiest namespace of all; apsivex.com squatted 2026-06 (irrelevant on our subdomain) |
| **Ogivon** | ogive + -on, coined | Soft/hard-g split; distant pharma neighbor Ogivri |
| **MMR Sim** | House-brand compound | "MMR sim" = gaming matchmaking-rating simulators in search, permanent SEO pollution; five spoken syllables |

Usable mediums (fallbacks): Meridian (clean in hobby, diluted everywhere), Thravex
(Trivex/Travex speech confusion), Apodyne (autocorrects to "anodyne", misreads as
Apogee), Azimuth + Semispan (collide with our own input fields), Trajex (TrajEx tool +
OMRON Trajexia).

**Claude's recommendation: Midchord** (real engineering quantity the kernel literally
computes; perfect say-once-spell-right; total freedom-to-operate — thin mark acceptable
for a free GPL tool), **challenger Ogivane** (most ownable with real rocketry meaning).
