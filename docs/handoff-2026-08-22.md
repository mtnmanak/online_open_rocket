## What this session did

and errors, clean up the git repos, debug the whole app, refactor for efficiency"),

The audit was an 11-dimension multi-agent pass with adversarial verification of

### v0.053 — crashes, data loss, a11y, CI
- **146 bundled motors publish no mass**, one lists more propellant than loaded
  mass. Picking **Estes 1/2A6** or **Klima B2** — both visible in the default list
  — threw a raw TeaVM BigInt error and blanked the entire design, and session
  autosave persisted it so a reload reproduced it. Guarded in three layers
  (thrustcurve refuses and names the motor, `hasMassData` greys the row,
  `assertFiniteCurve` guards the engine boundary) plus a data-integrity test over
  the committed `motors.json`.
- **RockSim fins on a transition** and **RASAero zero-tip-chord fins** imported
  cleanly then failed to build — no mass, CG, CP or Simulate. Both reproduce with
  fixtures already in the repo.
- **RASAero transitions imported as cylinders**: `<Diameter>` duplicates the REAR
  diameter, the fore radius is implicit in the preceding part.
- **Saving a multi-config `.ork` rewrote every OTHER configuration's recovery
  deployment.** A chute set for apogee could come back deploying at 300 m. Real
  recovery hazard; every configuration's deployment now round-trips.
- Mobile: the fixed tab bar permanently covered the site footer including
  **"Bugs & requests"**. 701–980 px: canvas overlays escaped onto the page header.
- **Escape closes every dialog and focus is managed** — there was not one Escape
  handler or one `.focus()` call in the whole app before this.
- **CI runs `npm test` before deploying** (it never did), and `npm test` now works
  on a fresh clone.

### v0.054 — bundle, fidelity, contrast, labels
- **three.js left the initial bundle: 835 → 578 kB gzip (−31 %).** Rocket3D is
  lazy and the 3D exporters load on click; the last holdout was one
  `ShapeUtils.triangulateShape` call in `solidMesh`, now behind its own module.
  Verified in a browser: the Design tab fetches no three; clicking 3D loads it and
  mounts a live WebGL context.
- Imported RockSim chutes weighed **2.9× too much** (bulk density × thickness was
  never converted to surface density); exported ones weighed **nothing**.
- `.ork` now PRESERVES fin fillets and `<instancecount>` instead of destroying
  them on save, with an import note saying they are not yet counted in mass.
- Three contrast pairs failing AA, worst the "Motor mount:" label at **1.41:1 in
  Daylight** — the mode built for sunlight. Every input and dropdown now has an
  accessible name.

### v0.055 → v0.058 — the click-to-select bug
Eric: clicking a nose cone or body tube in the horizontal 2D view never selected
it in the tree; other components were fine.

**I shipped two wrong fixes first.** Both were real defects — a `dragMoved` latch
that permanently killed those clicks after any drag (0.055), and CG/CP markers
absorbing clicks on the centreline (0.056) — but neither was the reported bug. I
"reproduced" it with synthesized clicks, which press and release at the **identical
coordinate**.

The real cause: **`onMove` panned the canvas on the FIRST pixel of movement, with
no threshold.** A real click jitters 1–3 px, so the drawing slid out from under the
pointer between press and release and the click landed on the `<svg>`. Children
were unaffected because `beginDrag` stops propagation; **vertical was unaffected
because it attaches no pan handler at all — which is the clue Eric handed me and
the thing that cracked it.** Fixed in v0.058 with a 4 px slop plus lazy pointer
capture. v0.058 also makes a failed image export report itself instead of silently
doing nothing.

## Two lessons worth keeping

1. **A synthesized click is not a click.** CDP `left_click` has zero jitter and
   cannot reproduce anything that depends on movement between press and release.
   Use `left_click_drag` over 2–3 px to model a real one.
2. **When the reporter's conditions do not match your reproduction, your
   reproduction is wrong.** Eric said it happened with no prior drag; my repro
   needed one. I shipped anyway and it cost two rounds.

Also: **real CDP input only reaches the page when the Chrome window is focused.**
When clicks produce no events at all, that is why — it is not the app.
