## Hard-won lessons (do not relearn)

- **The schematic must never size its own container.** TreeSchematic's adaptive
  height feeds off its container measure; putting it in a normal-flow flexing box
  created a measure→draw→grow loop that reached 2900px. The fix: `.hero-view` /
  `.fly-view` are `position:absolute` boxes (owned by App/FlyScreen — the
  schematic's wrap carries inline positioning that beats external CSS), inside
  FIXED-height stages.
- The S2 margin callout lives in the LOWER lane for a reason (control strip owns
  the upper-right); its collision nudge clears the CP label with a leftward
  fallback. Tests pin the exact numbers.
- 3D sprite labels: `center` offsets are SCREEN-space — CG above, CP below, margin
  right, or they pile up exactly when the margin is small (the case that matters).
