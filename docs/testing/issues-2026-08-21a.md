# OpenRocket Online — Notes & Feature Requests
**Date:** 21 August 2026 

I signed up for a RocketForge account at https://rocketforge.space/ and checked out their product. I want to be clear - i do not see them as a "competetor" - there is no "competition" happening. Neiter our tool or their tool or Open Rocket are looking to make money, these are all free apps. There is room for all of us here and i want to be friendly with all the people in this space
One note on the list of things to do, I have been talking to the people that have developed Open Rocket - they are friends of mine - so, even though there is no email chain, I have been keeping them informed as a courtesy and will continue to do so
Comparing the rocketforge product to ours is helpful in many ways. 
They are similar in the intent - both browser based 6DOF rocket sims, based of Open Rocket. 
However, RocketForge appears to be targetting classroom settings and team collaboration, while we are striving for a more advanced rocket sim that handles a lot of parts, higher end physics and more complicated designs. I see both apps exisitng very cleanly. RocketForge appears to be a great tool for a quick and easy sim, while our tool provides a depth RocketForge is not striving to achieve. That is great for all users.
I put some screenshots of RocketForge in the docs folder under a folder called "RocketForge Screenshots"
things i like about rocketforge we should think about
- very clean design - love the use of the large visual of the rocket taking up most of the screenshots
- they use large the large desktop browser window space VERY well - since the visual of the rocket is the main part of the interface, it looks very good and is more useable than our small rocket visual
- they depict parts and internals in a very visually appealing ways
- they overcome the desktop browser vs mobile issue by using a completely different interface on the mobile version. instead of trying to just compress things on mobile, the phone version basically just shows the visual of the rocket and then all interaction is through pop-menus accessed from menu bars at the top or bottom of the screen. elegant solution, if we do nothing else, we should thik about whether the horizontal view of the rocke in our app should be able to be flipped and should the view default to vertical on mobile
- i really like their "tour" tool tips that pop-up when a new user first uses the product. elegant quick start walk through

- things i don't like about rocketforge
- it has far fewer features and parts than our model
- the need to create an account and login - worse, the program auto logs the users out in just a few minutes, so, if you work on something else for just a little while, you have to log in again. Worse, the dashboard doesn't have a graceful degradation - if the app logs you out in the background, when you try to work in the editor or dashboard again, your work appears to be gone and you feel like you lost everything without realizing you have been logged out
- as far as I can tell, their product is not open source
- it is buggy, it crashed a couple times while i was using it
