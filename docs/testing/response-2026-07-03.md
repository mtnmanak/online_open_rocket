## Bug found & fixed along the way

Saving a **Haack or power-series nose to .ork silently reshaped it** — the writer
hardcoded `shapeparameter 1.0`, but the engine's default for Haack is 0 (Von Kármán)
and power is 0.5. Desktop would load your saved design with a different nose shape
(different CG/CP). Caught by extending the desktop-loader parity check to CG, fixed,
and now regression-tested. Your old exports of Haack noses were affected; re-save.
