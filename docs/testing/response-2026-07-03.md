## Bug found & fixed along the way

Saving a **Haack or power-series nose to .ork silently reshaped it** — the writer
hardcoded `shapeparameter 1.0`, but the engine's default for Haack is 0 (Von Kármán)
and power is 0.5. Desktop would load your saved design with a different nose shape
(different CG/CP). Caught by extending the desktop-loader parity check to CG, fixed,
and now regression-tested. Your old exports of Haack noses were affected; re-save.

## Motor selection rewrite (requested in follow-up discussion) — **Done**

- **Bundled motor database:** the complete thrustcurve.org catalog (1,129 motors,
  every certified motor incl. OOP) ships with the app as summary data — filtering
  and sorting are instant and offline; only the thrust curve downloads when you
  load a motor (then it's cached). `npm run motors:refresh` regenerates the bundle.
- **"Browse motor database…"** in the Motor panel opens the browser:
  - **Manufacturer toggles** (with per-maker motor counts), multi-select.
  - **Diameter-class toggles** — only classes that fit the mount appear (adapter
    logic: a 38 mm mount shows 38/29/24/18/13, never 54). **75 mm and 76 mm are one
    class ("75/76")** — AeroTech-vs-Loki casing rounding handled, with ~1.5 mm
    snap tolerance generally and ~1 mm bore clearance on the mount.
  - **Include out-of-production** toggle (off by default).
  - **Max motor length** input (in your motor-dimension units, click-to-change):
    longer motors are ⚠-flagged and greyed but still selectable.
  - Free-text designation search.
  - **Sortable table** — click any header: designation, maker, diameter, length,
    burn time (s), total impulse (Ns). Defaults to impulse, highest first.
  - Filter/sort state persists between sessions.
- Quick picks (built-in offline motors) stay in the sidebar for instant defaults.
- **Dark theme is now the default.** If you already saved preferences, your
  stored choice wins — flip it in ⚙ Preferences.
