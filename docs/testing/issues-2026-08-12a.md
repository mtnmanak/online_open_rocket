### The component STL exporter 

- i downloaded some components and they loaded in my slicer (Bambu Studio) with no issues
- this brings up another issues - many parts are too big for most 3D printers out there. My Bambu Labs H2D Printer is pretty big,. but probably couldn't even print a full nosecone for a 3" rocket. What I normally do in Fusion 360 or SolidWorks is split the part and then design it to print the separate parts with a method of reassembling the parent part after printing. For example, a nosecone can easily be split in half and the two halves can fit back together with a simple shoulder design. I am not sure if it is possible to offer to split parts for users (we would have to ask for their printer volume X,Y, and Z measurements) or if that is simply beyond the scope of what we can provide and they would just have to deal with it. Give me your thoughts.
